## Business Problem

Wanjiku runs a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market. For the last two weeks she has recorded her daily sales in a notebook and kept a rough record of her stock. She wants to know how her business is doing, whether it is growing, what she should restock, and where she should focus her effort. This project uses core Python only (no pandas or NumPy) to analyse her 14 days of sales (KES 46,450 in total) and her five-product stock list, and to turn the results into clear recommendations.

## Top Three Findings

1. **Sales are growing:** week 1 sales were KES 22,050 and week 2 were KES 24,400, a **10.66%** week-on-week increase.
2. **Weekends are the money days:** Saturday and Sunday average **KES 4,900** a day against **KES 2,685** on weekdays, which is **82.50% higher**.
3. **Two products need restocking now:** Tomatoes (18 kg vs a 25kg reorder level) and Sukuma (9 kg vs 15kg). Avocado has the best gross margin at **42.86%**.
